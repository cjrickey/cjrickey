# LinkedIn Company Page Kit — Schedule Narrative Generator

Everything to stand up the LinkedIn Company Page in ~10 minutes. An agent can't
create the page for you (it needs your logged-in LinkedIn account, and LinkedIn
blocks automated access — attempting it risks the account). So this is the
copy-and-paste kit; you create the page and drop these in.

## Steps

1. Go to linkedin.com → the **For Business** menu (grid icon, top right) → **Create a Company Page** → **Company**.
2. Enter the **Name** and **Website** above; LinkedIn generates the public URL (adjust if you like).
3. Set **Industry**, **Company size**, and confirm you're authorized to create the page.
4. After creation, open the page → **Edit** and paste the **Tagline**, **About**, and **Specialties**.
5. Upload `linkedin-profile-400.png` as the page **logo** and `linkedin-banner-1128x191.png` as the **cover**.
6. Publish. Then post the week's LinkedIn draft from `marketing/content/`.

Note: LinkedIn overlays the page logo near the lower-left of the cover on some
layouts — the banner here keeps its content centered, clear of that corner.
